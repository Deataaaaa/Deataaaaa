# The owner's benchmark reel: "What if neutrons disappeared for 5 seconds?"

10M views on TikTok (the creator's series: "What if electrons / neutrons disappeared for x seconds?").
80.0 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, no voiceover. Analysed frame by frame (2 fps sheets) and by spectrogram/EBU R128.

## Structure (3 hard cuts in 80 s, each on a big sound hit)
| Time | Shot | What happens | HUD (left, ~47% height) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | slow drone glide over Central Park at a pink dusk | title + 3D atom icon above it, ambient, nothing happens yet | `NEUTRONS DISAPPEAR IN 0:08.000` counting down in real time, sub `EVERY ATOM BUT HYDROGEN HAS THEM` |
| 8 s | **cut + hit** | | `NEUTRONS RETURN IN 0:04.910`, sub `TIME SLOWED DOWN 11×` |
| 8–44 s | one long take, the camera drifts through the park | atoms lose half their mass → POV hands rise (you weigh as much as a 5-year-old) → aurora-like light curtains → glowing hexagonal cracks spread over the ground → white-out | sub changes per beat: `YOUR WEIGHT: 41 LB`, `EVERY GRAM: 100 TONS OF TNT` |
| 44 s | **cut + hit** to space | Earth cracks, flashes, a fireball with shock rings expands | `EARTH IS NOW 17,553 MILES WIDE` |
| 52–64 s | slow push-in | the glowing remnant swells, the Moon is hit (dark disc in front) | `MOON HIT IN 7.9 S`, countdown reaches 0:00.023 |
| 64 s | **cut + white flash** | the city rebuilt out of gold particles ("Every neutron returns to where it was"), then it collapses into a swirl ("Alone, they fall in and turn to hydrogen") → beige flash → black | `NEUTRONS BACK`, sub `CENTRAL PARK, 5 SECONDS LATER` |
| 73.5–80 s | end card on #111 | the exact opening title again + 2 mono sublines (`NEUTRONS ARE 45% OF YOUR MASS / WITHOUT THEM, THE ONLY ELEMENT IS HYDROGEN`) | — |

## Why it works
1. **The premise is the hook**: a tiny, precise change ("for 5 seconds") with a gigantic consequence. The title stays on screen
   for the whole first 8 s.
2. **A countdown is the spine**: from frame 1 a millisecond timer runs to the event, then a second timer (slowed down 11×)
   runs to the end. The viewer always knows where they are and that a payoff is coming at zero (open loop).
3. **Escalation of scale every ~8–10 s**: your body → the people around you → the ground → the planet → the Moon → rebirth.
4. **Long continuous takes**: slow, eased drone/dolly moves; almost no cutting. The 3 cuts land on sound hits.
5. **One cohesive art direction**: stylised low-poly diorama, but gorgeous light (dusk, long soft shadows), bloom, sparkles,
   glowing cracks, particles. Faceless blocky people: no uncanny valley. One palette per section (pink dusk → orange glow →
   white → space black/orange → gold particles).
6. **Premium, quiet typography**: serif title (white, key word coloured), small serif-italic captions at ~2/3 height
   (one line, ~35–45 characters), tiny letter-spaced sans/mono HUD with the timer in serif digits. All text sits in the
   left/centre middle band, clear of the Instagram/TikTok UI (bottom 20%, right 15%).
7. **Numbers everywhere**: concrete, shareable figures in the HUD sublines (41 lb, 100 tons of TNT, 27,000 miles a second).
8. **Sound = cinematic score, no voice**: a sustained mid-range drone (~500–900 Hz, audible on phones), music-box/celesta
   notes in the calm part, tick pulses that get denser toward zero, a riser + a falling "downer" into every cut, a big
   broadband hit on the cut, hush on the end card. −16.1 LUFS integrated, LRA 14.5 LU, true peak −2.5 dBFS: quiet setup
   (−25 LUFS momentary), climax −10.
9. **Loop ending**: the end card repeats the opening title, so the replay starts seamlessly (rewatches, watch time > 100%).

## What we adopt (rule 17) and what we keep
- Adopt: the countdown spine, scale escalation, long eased takes with cuts on hits, golden-hour/dusk light with bloom and
  particles, the quiet serif typography (key word in our red `#ff2e2e`), HUD numbers, the score grammar, the loop end card.
- Keep: realism (rule 4), worst case + real cases (rule 12), the frame-1 shock (rule 14: the flash-forward now carries the
  title and the countdown), the loud phone master (rule 6: −10.5 LUFS, but with the reference's contrast between the calm
  setup and the climax), the end question (rule 15, written on the loop end card).
